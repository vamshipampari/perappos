Use this to:

- Log a product/business decision: add to docs/product.md under "Decisions" with date + rationale
- Update sprint status: edit docs/status.md
- Plan next features: update "Next" section in status.md
- Review what's done: summarize docs/status.md Done section

When logging a decision, always include:

- What was decided
- Why (the business/product reason)
- What alternatives were rejected and why
- Date

Reference docs/product.md and docs/status.md. These are the source of truth for product thinking.
